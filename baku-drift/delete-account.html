Delete your Avtosh: Baku Drift account

Avtosh: Baku Drift by Bahruz Shabili

In the game (immediate)

  1. Open the game and tap Settings.
  2. Open the Account tab and tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm. If you signed in a while ago, the game asks you to confirm your password, Apple or Google once more.

The account is deleted at once.

Without the game

If you no longer have the game, write to bahruzshabili@gmail.com from the e-mail address connected to your account (for an Apple or Google sign-in, the address that account uses) and include your username. We delete the account within 7 days and reply when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your username and account identifier
  • Your e-mail address and password hash, and your Apple and Google connections (the Sign in with Apple connection is also revoked at Apple)
  • Your saved progress and settings on our server
  • Your friends list, friend requests, blocks, recent players and the reports you made or that were made about you
  • All sign-in sessions and codes

Nothing is kept. Only the server's ordinary web logs (IP address and time) remain until they are erased automatically within 14 days. Deleting the account in the game also clears the progress on that phone.
