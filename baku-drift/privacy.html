Avtosh: Baku Drift — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Avtosh: Baku Drift ("the game") is made by Bahruz Shabili ("we"). This policy explains what information the game handles, why, and how you can delete it.

In short

  • We never sell your data. The game sends gameplay statistics only to our own server (no third-party analytics) to make the game better. The only ads are optional rewarded ads from Google AdMob, shown when you tap to watch one; see “Ads” for what Google receives.
  • When something goes wrong in the game (an error, a failed connection to a room, an invite that did not arrive), the game sends our server a short technical log so we can find and fix the problem. It is kept for 14 days.
  • An account is optional for playing alone. It keeps your cars and progress safe, and it is needed for online play (a guest account is enough).
  • We store a username, the sign-in method you choose and your game progress; if you play online, also your friends, the players you blocked and the players you recently raced with.
  • Other players can see your username, level and player number. The game has no chat.
  • You can delete your account at any time in the game; it is erased at once.
  • Gameplay statistics are erased after 180 days (or with your account).

Playing without an account

If you choose "Play as guest", your progress (in-game money, cars, parts, paint, best drift score) and your settings are kept on your phone. To keep a copy safe, the game also creates a guest account on our server that holds only a random identifier, a username and that progress. A guest account has no e-mail or name attached to you.

What an account stores

  • Username — the name shown in the game. You choose it and can change it at any time. It does not need to be your real name.
  • Sign-in method(s) you choose to connect:
    • E-mail and password — your e-mail address and a one-way hash of your password (we never store or see the password itself).
    • Sign in with Apple — the account identifier Apple gives us and the e-mail address Apple shares (this can be a private relay address if you choose "Hide My Email"). Apple also gives us a token we keep encrypted, only so we can tell Apple to disconnect the game when you delete your account.
    • Google — your Google account identifier and e-mail address. We do not receive your contacts, photos or anything else from Google.
  • Game progress and settings — the same save the game keeps on your phone, so it can come back on another device.
  • Sign-in sessions — for each device you are signed in on: when it was last used and the app's technical name (for example "UnityPlayer" and the platform). This lets us end a session when you sign out.

We do not ask for your real name, phone number, birthday, location, contacts or photos.

Online play

Online races and drift sessions, friends and invites need an account (a guest account is enough). This is what they use:

  • What other players see — your username, level and 8-digit player number, your car with its paint and parts, and where your car is during a race. Players who look you up by name or number also see whether you are online, in a room or racing. There is no text or voice chat.
  • Friends — friend requests and your friends list (up to 200).
  • Recent players — the last 30 players you raced with online, so you can add them as friends or invite them again.
  • Invites — an invite to a room carries your username and the room code and runs out after 60 seconds. If you mute a player's invites, that is kept for the minutes you choose (at most an hour).
  • Blocks — the players you block. A blocked player can't find you, invite you, send you friend requests or be matched with you.
  • Reports — when you report a player we store who reported whom, the reason you chose and your optional note (up to 200 characters). We use reports to remove offensive names and players who cheat or abuse others.
  • Online status and matchmaking — while you are online our server keeps in memory whether you are online, in a room or racing, and while you search for a quick match your level and the chosen mode. This is not saved and is forgotten within a minute after you leave.
  • Race connection — the cars' movement is passed between players through Unity Relay servers, so players never see each other's IP address. To pick the nearest server the game measures the ping to Unity's servers. For this, Unity Authentication gives the game an anonymous identifier that is not linked to your name or e-mail.
  • Same Wi-Fi rooms — a room on your local network connects the phones directly, and the host's local IP address is shown so the others can join. This traffic stays in your network.

Diagnostic logs

To find and fix problems, the game sends our server short technical log lines about what it was doing: errors and warnings from the game, joining and leaving online rooms (with the reason if it failed), invites sent and answered, the start and end of a round with its frame rate, which copy of your progress was kept when the phone and the server differed, and the screens loaded.

  • With each batch - the app version, your phone's model, operating system, memory and graphics chip, the game's language, and a random number for that run of the app.
  • Linked to your account - if you are signed in (a guest account counts), so we can look into a problem you tell us about; otherwise without an account.
  • Not included - no e-mail addresses (they are blanked out before sending), no passwords or tokens, nothing you type, no location and no advertising ID.
  • Kept for 14 days, then erased automatically; deleted at once with your account.

Gameplay statistics

To see what players enjoy and where the game needs work, the game sends our own server short records of how it is played. No third-party analytics service is used, and these records are never sold or shared.

  • What - the screens you open and how long you stay; each round (mode, map, car and its look, result and place, how long it lasted, speed, distance, drifts, two-wheel rides, crashes, resets, frame rate and battery use); what you try on and buy in the garage; shop, box, mission, ad and purchase events; settings you change; and your answers if you reply to the game's short "do you like it?" question.
  • About the phone - its model, operating system, memory, graphics chip, screen size, language, app version and the country (Cloudflare's guess from the connection; the IP address itself is not stored with these records).
  • Linked to - a random number the game makes on its first start (not the advertising ID), and to your account if you are signed in (a guest account counts).
  • Kept for 180 days, then erased automatically; deleted at once with your account.

Why we use it

Only to sign you in, keep your progress on our server, let you connect several sign-in methods to one account, run online play (matchmaking, friends, invites, blocks and reports) and send the e-mails you ask for (confirming your address or resetting your password). The legal basis is providing the service you asked for; keeping online play safe with blocks and reports is our legitimate interest. Ads: to show the rewarded ads you choose to watch; in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland personalised ads are shown only with your consent.

Ads

The game shows rewarded ads from Google AdMob only when you choose to watch one — for a discount in the shop or to get back into an offline Survive game. There are no ads you have to watch.

  • What Google receives — when the game starts and when an ad is loaded or shown, the Google Mobile Ads SDK sends Google your device's advertising ID (on Android), your IP address (from which an approximate location is worked out), device and app information (such as model, operating system, language and app version), diagnostic data and how you interact with the ad. Google uses it to show and measure ads, to prevent fraud and to improve its services.
  • Consent — in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland the game shows Google's consent form before ads are personalised. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Game → Ad consent.
  • iPhone — the game does not ask for permission to track you, so on iPhone ads are not personalised with your advertising identifier.
  • Advertising ID — on Android you can reset it or turn off ad personalisation in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • We do not receive or store this information ourselves: it goes from the SDK straight to Google. How Google uses data from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Purchases

Coins, boxes and the name coupon can be bought with real money. Payment is made entirely by Google Play or the App Store: we never see or store your card or payment details.

  • What we store — for each purchase: the store, the product, the store's order or transaction number, Google's purchase token, the time and the game version, linked to your account (if you are signed in).
  • Why — to give you what you bought (also on another phone signed in to the same account), to make sure one purchase counts only once and only for the account that made it, and to deal with refunds and payment fraud. The purchase token is checked with Google Play.
  • How long — as long as the account exists. If you delete your account, the purchase record is kept without any link to you, as sales records must be kept for accounting.
  • Random items — boxes contain random items; the odds of each item are shown in the game before you open or buy a box. Boxes are not sold in countries where this is not allowed.

Notifications

The game can remind you when your daily reward is ready and let you know when a player sends you a friend request, accepts yours or invites you to a room. It asks once, after your first game; you can turn notifications off at any time in Settings › Game or in your phone's settings.

  • Reminders — set on your phone itself (at most once a day, never at night) and taken away when you open the game. Nothing is sent to us for them.
  • Friend and invite notifications — if you are signed in (a guest account counts) and notifications are on, the game sends our server your phone's push token (an address for this app on this phone, given by Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging on Android or Apple's Push Notification service on iPhone), the platform and the game's language, linked to your account and that sign-in.
  • What a notification contains — only the other player's username and what happened (for example a friend request). Google or Apple delivers it to your phone. The same kind from the same player comes at most once in 10 minutes, and at most 20 a day.
  • How long — the token is deleted when you turn notifications off, sign out, delete your account or the sign-in ends (60 days without use), and when Google or Apple tells us it no longer works.

Services that take part

  • Apple and Google — only if you choose to sign in with them. Their own privacy policies apply to what you do on their sign-in screens.
  • Resend — delivers our e-mails (confirmation and password-reset codes); it receives your e-mail address and the message.
  • Cloudflare — protects and delivers the connection to our server.
  • Contabo — the company whose server (in France, EU) runs our account service and database.
  • Unity Gaming Services (Unity Technologies) — only when you play online: Authentication (an anonymous identifier), Relay (passes the race traffic between players; like any internet server it sees your IP address) and QoS (measures the ping to choose the nearest Relay server, usually in Europe). See Unity's privacy policy.
  • Google AdMob (Google) — shows the optional rewarded ads; see “Ads” above. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google) on Android and the Apple Push Notification service on iPhone — deliver the friend and invite notifications; see “Notifications” above. Only messaging is used: Firebase analytics is turned off.

Our server keeps ordinary web logs (IP address, time and the address requested) for up to 14 days to keep the service secure; they are not used for anything else.

How long we keep it

  • An account is kept until you delete it.
  • A guest account is erased automatically once it can no longer be opened — that is, when the game has not been opened with it for 60 days. Your progress stays on your phone.
  • Sign-in sessions end after 60 days without use; e-mail codes expire after 10 minutes.
  • Friends, blocks and recent players are kept until you remove them or delete your account; only the last 30 recent players are kept.
  • Reports are kept until the reporting or the reported account is deleted.
  • Invites run out after 60 seconds; online status and matchmaking data are kept only in memory while you play.
  • Diagnostic logs are erased after 14 days (or with your account).
  • A push token is deleted when notifications are turned off, on sign-out, with the sign-in or the account, or when it stops working.

Deleting your account

In the game open Settings → Account → Delete account. Your account, sign-in methods, sessions, saved progress, friends, friend requests, blocks, recent players and reports are erased from our server immediately and permanently, and a Sign in with Apple connection is revoked. Signing out or deleting the account also clears the progress on that phone. If you can no longer open the game, see how to delete your account.

Your rights

You can see your username and connected sign-in methods in the game, change your username, disconnect methods, remove friends, unblock players and delete everything. You can also write to us to ask for a copy of your data or for any other request about it.

Permissions

The game uses the internet for accounts and online play. On iPhone the system may ask for local network access when you use a same Wi-Fi room; it is used only to connect the phones in that room. The game does not ask for access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files. On Android the game declares the advertising ID permission (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID); it is used only by Google AdMob for ads. The game asks for permission to show notifications (Android 13 and newer, and iPhone) once, after your first game; if you say no, it works the same without them.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. It has no chat; optional purchases (coins, boxes, a name coupon) are made through Google Play or the App Store, where parents can require approval, and the odds of every box item are shown before buying; the only ads are optional rewarded ads from Google AdMob that a player chooses to watch. Online, other players see only your username, level and car, and any player can be blocked and reported. An account is not needed to play alone. If you believe a child has created an account and want it removed, write to us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated before the new version of the game is released, with a new date.
