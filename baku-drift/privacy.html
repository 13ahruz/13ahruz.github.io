Avtosh: Baku Drift — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Avtosh: Baku Drift ("the game") is made by Bahruz Shabili ("we"). This policy explains what information the game handles, why, and how you can delete it.

In short

  • The game has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and we never sell or share your data.
  • An account is optional for playing alone. It keeps your cars and progress safe, and it is needed for online play (a guest account is enough).
  • We store a username, the sign-in method you choose and your game progress; if you play online, also your friends, the players you blocked and the players you recently raced with.
  • Other players can see your username, level and player number. The game has no chat.
  • You can delete your account at any time in the game; it is erased at once.

Playing without an account

If you choose "Play as guest", your progress (in-game money, cars, parts, paint, best drift score) and your settings are kept on your phone. To keep a copy safe, the game also creates a guest account on our server that holds only a random identifier, a username and that progress. A guest account has no e-mail or name attached to you.

What an account stores

  • Username — the name shown in the game. You choose it and can change it at any time. It does not need to be your real name.
  • Sign-in method(s) you choose to connect:
    • E-mail and password — your e-mail address and a one-way hash of your password (we never store or see the password itself).
    • Sign in with Apple — the account identifier Apple gives us and the e-mail address Apple shares (this can be a private relay address if you choose "Hide My Email"). Apple also gives us a token we keep encrypted, only so we can tell Apple to disconnect the game when you delete your account.
    • Google — your Google account identifier and e-mail address. We do not receive your contacts, photos or anything else from Google.
  • Game progress and settings — the same save the game keeps on your phone, so it can come back on another device.
  • Sign-in sessions — for each device you are signed in on: when it was last used and the app's technical name (for example "UnityPlayer" and the platform). This lets us end a session when you sign out.

We do not ask for your real name, phone number, birthday, location, contacts or photos.

Online play

Online races and drift sessions, friends and invites need an account (a guest account is enough). This is what they use:

  • What other players see — your username, level and 8-digit player number, your car with its paint and parts, and where your car is during a race. Players who look you up by name or number also see whether you are online, in a room or racing. There is no text or voice chat.
  • Friends — friend requests and your friends list (up to 200).
  • Recent players — the last 30 players you raced with online, so you can add them as friends or invite them again.
  • Invites — an invite to a room carries your username and the room code and runs out after 60 seconds. If you mute a player's invites, that is kept for the minutes you choose (at most an hour).
  • Blocks — the players you block. A blocked player can't find you, invite you, send you friend requests or be matched with you.
  • Reports — when you report a player we store who reported whom, the reason you chose and your optional note (up to 200 characters). We use reports to remove offensive names and players who cheat or abuse others.
  • Online status and matchmaking — while you are online our server keeps in memory whether you are online, in a room or racing, and while you search for a quick match your level and the chosen mode. This is not saved and is forgotten within a minute after you leave.
  • Race connection — the cars' movement is passed between players through Unity Relay servers, so players never see each other's IP address. To pick the nearest server the game measures the ping to Unity's servers. For this, Unity Authentication gives the game an anonymous identifier that is not linked to your name or e-mail.
  • Same Wi-Fi rooms — a room on your local network connects the phones directly, and the host's local IP address is shown so the others can join. This traffic stays in your network.

Why we use it

Only to sign you in, keep your progress on our server, let you connect several sign-in methods to one account, run online play (matchmaking, friends, invites, blocks and reports) and send the e-mails you ask for (confirming your address or resetting your password). The legal basis is providing the service you asked for; keeping online play safe with blocks and reports is our legitimate interest.

Services that take part

  • Apple and Google — only if you choose to sign in with them. Their own privacy policies apply to what you do on their sign-in screens.
  • Resend — delivers our e-mails (confirmation and password-reset codes); it receives your e-mail address and the message.
  • Cloudflare — protects and delivers the connection to our server.
  • Contabo — the company whose server (in France, EU) runs our account service and database.
  • Unity Gaming Services (Unity Technologies) — only when you play online: Authentication (an anonymous identifier), Relay (passes the race traffic between players; like any internet server it sees your IP address) and QoS (measures the ping to choose the nearest Relay server, usually in Europe). See Unity's privacy policy.

Our server keeps ordinary web logs (IP address, time and the address requested) for up to 14 days to keep the service secure; they are not used for anything else.

How long we keep it

  • An account is kept until you delete it.
  • A guest account is erased automatically once it can no longer be opened — that is, when the game has not been opened with it for 60 days. Your progress stays on your phone.
  • Sign-in sessions end after 60 days without use; e-mail codes expire after 10 minutes.
  • Friends, blocks and recent players are kept until you remove them or delete your account; only the last 30 recent players are kept.
  • Reports are kept until the reporting or the reported account is deleted.
  • Invites run out after 60 seconds; online status and matchmaking data are kept only in memory while you play.

Deleting your account

In the game open Settings → Account → Delete account. Your account, sign-in methods, sessions, saved progress, friends, friend requests, blocks, recent players and reports are erased from our server immediately and permanently, and a Sign in with Apple connection is revoked. Signing out or deleting the account also clears the progress on that phone. If you can no longer open the game, see how to delete your account.

Your rights

You can see your username and connected sign-in methods in the game, change your username, disconnect methods, remove friends, unblock players and delete everything. You can also write to us to ask for a copy of your data or for any other request about it.

Permissions

The game uses the internet for accounts and online play. On iPhone the system may ask for local network access when you use a same Wi-Fi room; it is used only to connect the phones in that room. The game does not ask for access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Children

The game has no ads, no in-app purchases and no chat. Online, other players see only your username, level and car, and any player can be blocked and reported. An account is not needed to play alone. If you believe a child has created an account and want it removed, write to us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated before the new version of the game is released, with a new date.
