Questions

How do I drift?
Turn into a corner, tap the handbrake lever, then counter-steer and keep the gas pedal down. Longer slides at a bigger angle score more points, and points turn into in-game money.
How do I reverse?
Slide the gear lever up to R and press the gas. If you are still rolling forward, the car brakes first and then drives backwards.
How do I get new cars and parts?
Earn money by drifting and racing, then buy cars in the Shop and paint, rims, stance, tint and performance parts in the Garage. There are no real-money purchases.
I lost my progress.
Progress is saved on your phone only. Deleting the game, or clearing its data, removes it.
The game runs slowly on my phone.
Open Settings and set Graphics to Low.
