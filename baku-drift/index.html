Questions

How do I drift?
Turn into a corner, tap the handbrake lever, then counter-steer and keep the gas pedal down. Longer slides at a bigger angle score more points, and points turn into in-game money.
How do I reverse?
Slide the gear lever up to R and press the gas. If you are still rolling forward, the car brakes first and then drives backwards.
How do I get new cars and parts?
Earn money by drifting and racing, then buy cars in the Shop and paint, rims, stance, tint and performance parts in the Garage. There are no real-money purchases.
How do I keep my progress on a new phone?
Open Settings → Account and connect Apple, Google or an e-mail and password. On the new phone, sign in with the same method and your cars and money come back.
I forgot my password.
On the e-mail sign-in screen tap "Forgot password?" and enter the code we send to your e-mail.
How do I delete my account?
Settings → Account → Delete account. It is erased at once. See deleting your account.
The game runs slowly on my phone.
Open Settings and set Graphics to Low.

Privacy

No ads, no analytics, no tracking. An optional account stores only a username, the sign-in method you choose and your game progress. Read the privacy policy.
