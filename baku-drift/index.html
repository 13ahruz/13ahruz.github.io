Avtosh: Baku Drift

Drift and race classic cars through the streets of Baku.

Contact

Found a bug or have an idea? Write to bahruzshabili@gmail.com and tell us your phone model if something looks wrong.

Questions

How do I drift?
Turn into a corner, tap the handbrake, then counter-steer and keep the gas pedal down. Longer slides at a bigger angle score more points, and points turn into in-game money.
How do I reverse?
Stop, then keep the brake pressed: after a moment the car starts reversing. Press the gas to drive forward again.
How do I get new cars and parts?
Earn money by drifting and racing, then buy cars in the Shop and paint, rims, stance, tint and performance parts in the Garage. There are no real-money purchases.
How do I keep my progress on a new phone?
Open Settings → Account and connect Apple, Google or an e-mail and password. On the new phone, sign in with the same method and your cars and money come back.
I forgot my password.
On the e-mail sign-in screen tap "Forgot password?" and enter the code we send to your e-mail.
How do I play online with friends?
Open Online race or Online drift (you need an account — a guest account is enough). Quick match finds players close to your level and fills empty places with bots. Or create a room and invite friends or players you raced with recently, or share the room code. Add friends by their name or 8-digit player number in Friends. On the same Wi-Fi you can play without internet.
How do I block or report a player?
Tap ••• next to the player in the room or in your Friends list and choose Block or Report. A blocked player can't invite you, send you friend requests or be matched with you. We review reports and remove offensive names and players who cheat or abuse others.
How do I change the language?
Settings → Game → Language. The game speaks Azerbaijani, English, Russian and Turkish.
How do I delete my account?
Settings → Account → Delete account. It is erased at once. See deleting your account.
The game runs slowly on my phone.
Open Settings → Game and set Graphics to Low.

Privacy

No ads, no analytics, no tracking. An optional account stores a username, the sign-in method you choose, your game progress and, if you play online, your friends list. Read the privacy policy.
