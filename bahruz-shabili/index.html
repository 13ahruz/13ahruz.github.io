Bahruz Shabili is an independent content creator that produces illustrated, narrated videos about business ideas, entrepreneurship and practical money topics for an Azerbaijani-speaking audience.

About the Bahruz Shabili Publisher app

Bahruz Shabili Publisher is a private, single-operator tool that the channel owner runs on their own computer. It only publishes videos that the owner has produced to the owner's own accounts:

  • YouTube – uploads finished videos and Shorts, and sets their title, description and thumbnail, using the YouTube Data API.
  • TikTok – uploads short vertical clips to the owner's TikTok account, using the TikTok Content Posting API.

The app is not offered to the public, does not have other users and does not collect data from viewers.
