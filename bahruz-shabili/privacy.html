Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Bahruz Shabili Publisher (“the app”, “we”) handles information. The app is a private tool operated by the owner of the Bahruz Shabili channel to publish their own videos to their own YouTube and TikTok accounts. It has no other users.

1. Information the app accesses

  • Google / YouTube: after the account owner signs in with Google OAuth, the app receives an access token and refresh token with the scopes needed to upload videos and manage their metadata and thumbnails (youtube.upload, youtube). It may read the channel ID, channel title and the list of videos uploaded by the app.
  • TikTok: after the account owner signs in with TikTok Login Kit, the app receives an access token and refresh token, the account's open ID and basic profile information (display name, avatar), and uses the video.upload / video.publish scopes to post videos.

The app does not access viewers' data, comments' authors, contacts, messages, payment information or any data of people other than the account owner.

2. How the information is used

The tokens and account identifiers are used only to upload videos created by the account owner to the owner's own channels, and to set the title, description, tags, privacy status and thumbnail of those videos. The information is not used for advertising, profiling, analytics resale or any other purpose.

3. Storage and security

Tokens are stored locally on the owner's personal computer and are never sent to any server other than Google's and TikTok's official APIs. We do not operate a backend server or database, and we do not sell, rent or share this information with any third party.

4. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while the owner uses the app and can be deleted at any time by removing the local token files. Access can also be revoked at any time:

  • Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions
  • TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions

5. Google API Services and YouTube API Services

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services; by using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

6. TikTok

Content posted through the TikTok Content Posting API is subject to TikTok's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children and is used only by the adult channel owner.

8. Changes

We may update this policy; the date at the top shows the latest version.
