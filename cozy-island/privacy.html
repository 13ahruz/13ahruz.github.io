Cozy Island — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

Cozy Island ("the game") is made by JoinMe. This policy explains what information is handled when you play.

What the game itself stores

Your progress (levels, stars, chosen theme and sound settings) is saved only on your device. The game has no accounts, and we do not run servers that receive your data. Deleting the game deletes this information.

Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID, or Apple's IDFA if you allow tracking),
  • your IP address, from which an approximate location can be derived,
  • information about the ads you see and interact with,
  • diagnostic and performance information.

Google processes this information under its own policy: policies.google.com/privacy. How Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your choices

  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time under Settings → Privacy options in the game.
  • On iPhone, the game asks for permission to track. If you decline, ads are not personalised with your IDFA. You can change this in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
